About Vluchtdeals

Vluchtdeals is a Dutch service that finds unusually cheap flights from Amsterdam, Eindhoven, Brussels and Düsseldorf and shares them for free on Telegram, Instagram and TikTok. We do not sell tickets; travellers book directly with the airline or travel provider.

We create our own short videos and photo posts about these deals and publish them to our own Instagram and TikTok accounts through the platforms' official APIs, including TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. These integrations are used only to publish our own content to our own accounts. We do not collect data about other users.
